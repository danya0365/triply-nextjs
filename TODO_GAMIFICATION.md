# Triply - Gamification System

ระบบ Gamification ที่ออกแบบมาเพื่อสร้าง engagement และทำให้ผู้ใช้อยากกลับมาใช้บริการอย่างต่อเนื่อง

---

## 🎯 Gamification Overview

### Core Principles
1. **Make Travel More Rewarding** - เดินทางทุกครั้งได้คะแนนและรางวัล
2. **Encourage Planning** - วางแผนดีๆ ได้โบนัส
3. **Build Community** - แชร์ประสบการณ์ได้รางวัล
4. **Create Habit** - กิจกรรมประจำวันให้คะแนน
5. **Progress Visibility** - เห็นความก้าวหน้าชัดเจน

### Key Components
1. **Points System** (ระบบคะแนน)
2. **Levels & Tiers** (ระดับและชั้น)
3. **Missions** (ภารกิจ)
4. **Goals** (เป้าหมาย)
5. **Achievements** (ความสำเร็จ)
6. **Rewards** (รางวัล)
7. **Challenges** (ความท้าทาย)
8. **Leaderboards** (กระดานผู้นำ)

## 💰 Points System

### How to Earn Points

#### Booking Activities (40% of points)
- [ ] **Complete a Booking** - 100-500 points
  - Based on booking value
  - Formula: booking_value × 0.1
  - Bonus for first booking: +100 points

- [ ] **Early Booking Bonus** - 50 points
  - Book 30+ days in advance

- [ ] **Last-Minute Booking** - 30 points
  - Book within 3 days

- [ ] **Weekend Getaway** - 25 points
  - Book Friday-Sunday stays

- [ ] **Long Stay Bonus** - 100 points
  - Book 7+ nights

- [ ] **Multiple Room Booking** - 50 points per room
  - Book 2+ rooms in one transaction

#### Planning Activities (25% of points)
- [ ] **Create a Trip** - 20 points
  - First trip in account

- [ ] **Complete Trip Details** - 50 points
  - Fill all sections (accommodation, activities, budget)

- [ ] **Add 5+ Activities** - 30 points
  - Detailed planning

- [ ] **Create Budget Plan** - 25 points
  - Fill budget breakdown

- [ ] **Share Trip** - 15 points
  - Make trip public or share with friends

- [ ] **Collaborate on Trip** - 20 points
  - Invite co-planners

#### Social Activities (20% of points)
- [ ] **Write a Review** - 50 points
  - After stay completion
  - Bonus for photos: +25 points

- [ ] **Share Experience** - 30 points
  - Post on social feed

- [ ] **Upload Photos** - 10 points per photo
  - Max 50 points per trip

- [ ] **Helpful Review** - 5 points
  - Each "helpful" vote received

- [ ] **Refer a Friend** - 200 points
  - Friend completes first booking
  - Friend gets 100 points too

#### Daily/Engagement Activities (15% of points)
- [ ] **Daily Login** - 5 points
  - Consecutive days bonus:
    - 7 days: +35 points
    - 30 days: +150 points

- [ ] **Browse Destinations** - 2 points
  - View 5+ destination pages

- [ ] **Save Favorites** - 3 points
  - Add properties to wishlist

- [ ] **Complete Profile** - 100 points
  - One-time bonus

- [ ] **Enable Notifications** - 25 points
  - One-time bonus

### Points Redemption

- [ ] **Discount Vouchers**
  - 500 points = ฿50 voucher
  - 1,000 points = ฿120 voucher
  - 2,500 points = ฿300 voucher
  - 5,000 points = ฿700 voucher

- [ ] **Upgrades**
  - 1,500 points = Free room upgrade (subject to availability)
  - 2,000 points = Early check-in
  - 1,000 points = Late checkout

- [ ] **Perks**
  - 800 points = Free breakfast for 2
  - 1,200 points = Airport transfer
  - 3,000 points = Spa treatment voucher

- [ ] **Exclusive Access**
  - 5,000 points = VIP support for 1 year
  - 10,000 points = Exclusive property access

## 💡 Gamification Best Practices

### Do's ✅
- Make points easy to earn initially
- Provide clear progress visibility
- Celebrate all wins (big and small)
- Keep missions achievable
- Update challenges regularly
- Balance competition with cooperation
- Ensure rewards have real value

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't make it too complex
- Don't punish users with point loss
- Don't require excessive grinding
- Don't make rewards too expensive
- Don't spam notifications
- Don't favor whales only
- Don't forget casual users
